Who We Are

Amigo Pro ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website and platform available at amigo-pro.com, a digital presence platform that connects independent Hispanic service professionals ("Amigos") with clients ("Clientes") seeking local services. We are not a staffing agency, employer, or party to any transaction between Amigos and Clientes.

Contact: admin@amigo-pro.com
2

Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Account registration: full name, email address, city, state, ZIP code, phone number, professional title, and bio.
  • Profile content: profile photo, cover photo, portfolio images, services offered, pricing, and social media links.
  • Business data: clients, projects, appointments, notes, and financial records you enter into the CRM and finance modules.
  • Payment information: billing is processed by PayPal. We do not store your full card number or bank details. PayPal's Privacy Policy governs payment data.
  • Communications: messages sent through the in-platform chat between Amigos and Clientes.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data: pages visited, features used, time spent, button clicks, and navigation path.
  • Device and browser data: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, screen resolution, and referring URL.
  • Location data: approximate geolocation derived from IP address, and precise GPS coordinates if you grant browser permission when using the directory search.
  • Cookies and tracking technologies: see the Cookie Policy.

Information from third parties

  • Google OAuth: if you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account name, email address, and profile photo. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Ads: if you interact with our ads, Meta may share aggregate audience data with us under Meta's own privacy terms.
3

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the platform: create and display your public profile, run the directory, enable appointments and messaging.
  • To process payments: communicate with PayPal to create and manage your subscription.
  • To personalize your experience: show relevant results in the directory based on location and category.
  • To communicate with you: send transactional emails (account verification, appointment confirmations, subscription receipts), and optional marketing emails if you opt in.
  • To improve the platform: analyze usage patterns to fix bugs and develop new features.
  • To show advertising: we use Meta Pixel to measure the effectiveness of our ads on Facebook and Instagram. This may involve transferring anonymized audience data to Meta.
  • To protect the platform: detect fraud, spam, and abuse; enforce our Terms of Service.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

How We Share Your Information

Public information

Your Amigo profile — including your name, title, city, bio, services, portfolio images, and testimonials — is publicly visible on amigo-pro.com and may be indexed by search engines. You control which sections appear via your dashboard Design settings.

Service providers

We share data with third-party vendors who help us operate the platform, including:

  • SiteGround — web hosting and server infrastructure.
  • Cloudflare R2 — image storage and CDN delivery.
  • PayPal — payment processing and subscription management.
  • Google — OAuth login authentication.
  • Meta — advertising measurement (anonymized/aggregated data only).

Legal requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

Business transfers

If Amigo Pro is acquired, merged, or its assets are sold, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

We do not sell your personal data. Amigo Pro does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Your Rights — California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

Right to Know

You may request a copy of the personal information we have collected about you in the past 12 months.

Right to Delete

You may request that we delete your personal information, subject to certain exceptions.

Right to Opt-Out of Sale

We do not sell personal information. This right is satisfied by default.

Non-Discrimination

We will not deny you services or charge different prices because you exercised any of these rights.

To exercise your rights, email us at admin@amigo-pro.com with the subject line "CCPA Privacy Request". We will respond within 45 days.

All users — not just California residents — may update or delete their account at any time from Settings → Delete my account in the dashboard.
6

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your profile, CRM data, financial records, messages, and images within 30 days. Some data may be retained longer if required by law (e.g., billing records for 7 years per IRS guidance).

7

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data in transit, bcrypt password hashing, nonce-based CSRF protection, and role-based access controls. Hosting is on SiteGround GoGeek with automatic daily backups and Wordfence security active.

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.
8

Children's Privacy

Amigo Pro is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, contact us at admin@amigo-pro.com and we will delete it promptly.

9

International Users

Amigo Pro is operated from the United States. If you access the platform from outside the U.S. (e.g., Mexico or other Latin American countries), your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States. By using the platform, you consent to this transfer.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by email or by posting a prominent notice on the platform at least 30 days before the changes take effect. Continued use of the platform after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
